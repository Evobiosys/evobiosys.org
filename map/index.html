The Public Map

This is the living public map of EvoBioSys: holons (organizational cells, each whole in itself and part of a greater whole) and quests (things that ought to exist, open for anyone to pick up). Nothing here is final: holons grow and shift pillars, quests get claimed, statuses change as the work does.

States

One vocabulary, six states

Every holon and every quest on this page carries exactly one of these states.

  • Active in progress, being worked on right now
  • Draft written or built in a first pass, not yet finished
  • Vision imagined and scoped, not yet started
  • Legacy built before, now dormant or superseded
  • Identified captured, nobody working on it yet
  • Paused on hold
