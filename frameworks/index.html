9x9 Framework

EvoBioSys

Sovereign access to nine essential physical requirements across nine power poles - the floor on which all other investing stands. Two nines, deliberately parallel to the 99 names of Allah.

  1. Security (police, military, diplomacy)
  2. Water
  3. Food

  5. Clothing
  6. Energy (incl. heating)
  7. Transportation

  • US
  • EU
  • China
  • India
  • ASEAN
  • Sub-Saharan Africa
  • Latin America
  • Arab world
  • Central Asia

Every cell (essential × pole) is a deployment opportunity. Sovereignty here means: communities can participate in economic life on their own terms, without being blackmailable through any single dependency. The two nines parallel the 99 names of Allah - a structural rhyme, not a religious claim.

Deep dive: the 9x9 is the structural floor of EvoBioSys Capital's investment thesis. The thesis page walks through why sovereignty means unblackmailability, why the multi-pole posture is non-negotiable, and how the grid translates into deployment.

Naming note: an older formulation called this 949 (from 2023 Logseq notes). Going forward, use 9x9 consistently.
