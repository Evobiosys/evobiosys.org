## Content brief (all verified facts — use these, nothing else)

**Name:** EvoPaideia — the developmental-education holon of EvoBioSys. Evo (Latin/English: evolution, unfolding) + Παιδεία (Ancient Greek: the formation of the whole human being — character, civic virtue, self-governance). Sibling register: "Developmental Field" serves as the secular umbrella name (domains developmental-field.org/.com secured); "Academy" refers narrowly to a future gap-year program. This page leads with EvoPaideia and may note the umbrella name once.

**Two registers to BRIDGE (both current, deliberately):**
- Philosophical: "a holon for human development, not a school… a body of practice that can take many forms: a circle of peers, a mentoring relationship, a residency, a single well-held conversation."
- Investable: "a developmental field (an integral learning ecology) where young Europeans follow their desire first and earn an accredited degree second — resourced so no one carries debt, held by educators who each run a live project, built as a community of practice rather than a hierarchy."
Bridge them explicitly: the practice is the product; the accreditation path makes it legible.

**Problem:** modern education narrowed to credentialing and career preparation; the default continental bachelor's experience = "being a number in a large impersonal system." Wider why: civilization needs competent communities to act well through the metacrisis; "tertiary education is expensive, placeless-or-city-only, and tool-poor." Founder note: Jakob studied at Minerva — arrived when it was "already 90%-formed"; EvoPaideia is the thing he wanted access to.

**The model (core mechanisms):**
- Desire-first, degree-second: ~2 years studying what the learner actually wants, accumulating nano-degrees; then multi-factor mapping of accumulated learning to the closest accreditable degree at a partner university (example: ten coding-heavy nano-degrees sit one course from a BSc Computer Science, nine from a BA Art History). "Knowledge first; the degree turns out of it."
- Developmental field, not catalogue: "Rather than designing curricula that transmit information, we design fields — living environments that call forth development in the people who enter them." Grounded in Mutual Holarchic Sovereignty: "you grow in the creative tension between autonomy and belonging."
- Field-tending cycle: Meet (begin where the person actually is) → Hold (safety to risk the discomfort of growth) → Provoke (the right challenge at the developmental edge — "growth lives at the edge of the manageable") → Reflect (integration). A cycle, not a ladder.
- Educators: post-Humboldtian — every educator runs a live project and learns inside it; roles = mentors, space-holders, go-to people.
- Membership (Liminal Village Criteria, credit Roberto Valenti): work on self · work on an impact project · work for the community.
- SOUBR floor: free housing + sustenance (langar-inspired — the Sikh free-kitchen tradition), so neither students nor staff carry debt; a nominal ~€1k/year keeps it worth something. This answers the elite-enclave objection: "lead the mainstream, not the margin" (contrast Minerva's global-elite niche).
- Signature practice: the "Onion of Truth" — each participant articulates their worldview and life-direction as a revisable reference point.

**Container ladder (roadmap spine — this is your interactive demo material):** one call → one day → one weekend (remote) → one weekend in person → one week (Sun–Sat) → one month → … → one-year residency. Then: universities for accreditation mapping; then the incubator alongside the second cohort. Vienna base (night-train-connected locations), Netherlands second hub.

**First domino tie-in (proposed, not executed):** the nearest concrete container — a vacant building lent via *Prekarium* (gratuitous, revocable Austrian loan-for-use), foundation-held kit, food covered, 1–3 residents with real projects in a mutual developmental field. "The first EvoPaideia node can be the first housing node — one building, three pillars." Doable in weeks, no fund needed. Cross-link `/unlocking-housing/` (site page) for the housing pitch.

**Structure & capital (present as design, confidence explicitly moderate):** four bodies — Foundation/commons, teaching org, incubator, fund; mission stays sovereign, capital returns via incubator equity through the fund (explicitly NOT module licensing). Bootstrap year one (~€1k/participant, small founding cohort), then City of Vienna, philanthropy, family offices, EU programs (Erasmus flagged: needs research, grant-capture risk).

**Status (honest, verbatim-usable):** "deliberately early and practice-led… What exists now is the philosophy, the field-tending method, and Athena as the place where both are being tested in practice." Athena = the first sub-holon: designing and testing developmental fields for adults, deliberately small. No cohort has run; no building secured; no university LOI; legal vehicle = a Verein, foundable in about a week, when the first container lands. Track record: founder held founder + educator seats in the Integral Youth Gatherings (mention once, no invented details).

**Vision:** "Living systems can only evolve as far as the people within them have developed. If you want a more alive world, you need more fully developed people. Education is not preparation for life — it is the practice of life itself." Long horizon: a federated network of developmental hubs (Vienna first, Netherlands second); an arc from childhood to elderhood (horizon, not claim); "a living tradition, not a program" — handed on, renewed.

**Six principles (use as cards/chips):** Conditions over content · The whole person · Meet, don't force · Sovereignty and belonging · Relationship as medium · Depth over scale.

**Gold phrasings (use several, sparingly):** "We tend the soil rather than installing the plant." / "You cannot make a seed grow faster by pulling on it." / "The goal of education is not to produce knowledgeable people. It is to support the unfolding of human beings who can meet the complexity of their time." / "A tradition is not inherited by being told. It is inherited by being practised."

## Claims you must NOT make

- No cohort, building, university partnership, accreditation, or legal entity exists yet — never imply otherwise.
- IYG: one clause max, zero invented specifics (no dates/numbers).
- Don't promise degree accreditation — it's the mapped path after the residency milestone.
- Capital mechanics carry the thesis's own moderate confidence — frame as "designed, not raised."
- Do not use the name "EvoSattva"/"Polysattva" (internal registers); "Developmental Field" once as umbrella note is fine.
