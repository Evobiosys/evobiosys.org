Unlocking Housing

Open-source, local-first tools for discovering vacant and developable properties in Austria — built for bioregional communities, housing cooperatives, and people working to bring land back into use.

How it works Bio pillar

The problem it solves

Austria has significant stocks of vacant, underutilised, and potentially developable land — but finding it requires navigating fragmented cadastral databases, proprietary tools, and opaque administrative processes. The information exists; what’s missing is a way to work with it that is local, sovereign, and built for the communities who need it most.

Rather than building more, the starting question is: what is already here, and how can it be brought back into life?

Unlocking Housing is a suite of geo-mapping modules that runs on your own machine, connects to public Austrian spatial data sources, and produces actionable maps of where housing development may be possible — without sending your research to a third-party server.

Module suite

A SvelteKit monorepo organised into focused, independently usable modules.

map-core

Map Core

MapLibre GL JS base viewer, tile serving, and shared geo utilities.

geo-layers-at

Austrian Geo Layers

Adapters for Austrian open data: cadastre, planning zones, HORA flood risk, WMS endpoints.

vacancy

Vacancy Detection

Spatial heuristics for identifying potentially vacant or long-term non-used properties.

bioregions

Bioregions

Watershed, soil, and ecological connectivity overlays for bioregional context.

listings-matcher

Listings Matcher

Cross-reference property listings with cadastral data to surface discrepancies and opportunities.

writeback

Logseq Writeback

Export property investigations directly to your Logseq knowledge base.

The Bio pillar

Unlocking Housing is the flagship project of evoBIOsys-bio — the branch of EvoBioSys concerned with living, biological, and bioregional systems. Where the Sys pillar builds digital infrastructure and the Evo pillar supports human development, the Bio pillar works with the living substrate: land, housing, bioregions, and the material conditions of settlement.

What evoBIOsys-bio builds toward

The Bio pillar’s long-term work is to develop tools and frameworks for communities to understand, steward, and inhabit their bioregions more intentionally. Unlocking Housing is the first tool in that suite: starting with where can people live? and building toward how should communities relate to the land they occupy?

Every analysis in Unlocking Housing includes watershed context, soil data, and ecological connectivity — because these dimensions shape whether a development will be resilient over decades, not just permitted for next year.
