01 · The floor, not the ceiling

What a healthy ecosystem provides for free.

A forest doesn't charge its trees for water. A coral reef doesn't invoice its fish for shelter. 9EPhR asks a harder, simpler question than meaning or self-actualization: what are the material preconditions that must be met before any higher development becomes possible?

9 requirements
Organized in three rows of three, tracing how our species added them over time.
v0.3 working hypothesis
Explicitly open to refinement — an earlier version named only seven.
0 independent economic engine
By design. 9EPhR is a specification, not a service — funding flows through SoUBR/SoFin instead.

When someone is anxious about rent, food, or healthcare, the nervous system stays in survival mode. 9EPhR defines what "safe enough to contribute" looks like in physical terms.

02 · The nine requirements

Three eras, three rows, one floor.

Bodily base — what humans have always needed
1

Water

Safe, potable water for drinking, cooking, sanitation — a commons, not a commodity.

2

Food

Sufficient, diverse, nourishing food — real nutrition from regenerative agriculture and local systems.

3

Shelter

Safe, stable housing for rest, privacy, and regeneration — a biological necessity, not a financial asset.

Later additions — warmth, movement & covering
4

Energy

Clean, reliable energy for heating, cooling, cooking. Energy sovereignty underwrites every other requirement.

5

Transportation

The ability to move between places — public transit, walkable cities, cycling infrastructure.

6

Clothing

Appropriate clothing and bodily protection for climate and environment — the extended skin.

Modern guarantees — health, safety & connection
7

Hygiene & healthcare

Preventive and restorative care for the biological instrument — not a market service, a requirement.

8

Security

Physical safety and rule of law — policing, defense, diplomacy that protect people from violence and coercion.

9

Digital & connectivity

Access to communication and information infrastructure — as essential in this century as roads once were.

03 · Part of a triad

Not a hierarchy. A triad — each incomplete without the others.

9EPhR

The physical. What the body needs. The foundation layer — without it, nothing above stabilizes.

MHS

The relational. How we govern ourselves — nested sovereignty where autonomy reinforces autonomy at every scale.

Integral Theory

The developmental. How we understand reality — extension of natural sciences and big history, We-Space and sourcing. No holon page exists for this leg yet.

Physical requirements without sovereignty become welfare dependency. Sovereignty without integral development becomes ideology. Integral development without physical security becomes spiritual bypassing.

04 · The financial bridge

9EPhR names what. SoUBR funds how.

SoUBR (Sovereign Universal Basic Requirements), the financial scaffolding layer inside SoFin, is where the nine requirements translate into concrete financial mechanisms — targeted support that eliminates survival anxiety without creating dependency.

9EPhR is the conceptual framework: universal, abstract, applicable to any civilization. SoUBR is the operational layer: specific to the SoFin ecosystem. Think of 9EPhR as the specification, SoUBR as the implementation — and 9EPhR's real-world relevance stays unproven until SoUBR runs one full funded-and-returned cycle.

05 · Where it stands

A working hypothesis. No build track of its own.

Already real

  • Live holon page, v0.3 — the nine requirements, the triad framing, and the SoUBR bridge all published.
  • Consuming contexts named — EU policy, corporate ESG, coastal infrastructure, regenerative agriculture, Community Hubs, EvoPaideia.

Honestly missing

  • No code, no tool, no dataset — nothing to deploy beyond the page itself.
  • Not yet validated against one real consuming context (Feldbach or Integrales Wien) to see which of the nine hold up.
  • No monetization path for the framework itself — its relevance rides entirely on SoUBR/SoFin proving the model downstream.
Honest framing. 9EPhR sits at draft, one revision past initial idea. Its endpoint is conceptual maturity plus adoption by consuming holons — not deployment. Its "living system" state is unverifiable beyond the page itself until SoUBR operationalizes it.
