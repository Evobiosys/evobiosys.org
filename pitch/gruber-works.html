02 · The legal form

Societas Europae — genuinely European, not merely national.

The SE (Societas Europae, EU Council Regulation No. 2157/2001) is a company form recognized across every EU/EEA member state at once. It can transfer its registered office between member states without dissolving and re-incorporating — a portability no national company form matches.

For Gruber Works the SE is a deliberate choice: it aligns with EvoBioSys's sovereignty philosophy (EU data protection, EU corporate law, EU jurisdiction) and future-proofs the structure as holons expand across markets — at the cost of a higher setup bar than a freelancer platform.

04 · The bridge

Your creation's bridge to state institutions.

Creative and technological work does not naturally speak the language of state bureaucracy. Tax authorities want invoices with specific fields; banks want articles of incorporation; insurers want a legal person to underwrite. Gruber Works is designed to translate — without absorbing the holons it serves.

What the bridge would carry

Invoicing and VAT compliance, contractual standing, EU-regulated banking, liability containment, and regulatory filings — for any holon that needs one.

What it does not do

It does not absorb a holon's identity or operating logic. Each project keeps its own culture; Gruber Works only provides the surface state institutions recognize.

06 · The vision
