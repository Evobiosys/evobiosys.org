Issue #1 — Holon pages expand, 9EPhR corrected, Complete Chronology opens

This is Issue #1 of the EvoBioSys newsletter — a changelog of recent work across the holonic network, not a polished announcement. Three threads moved this cycle.

Four holon pages expand

Community Hubs, EvoPaideia, World Lore, and QuestHub all grew out, alongside a sync of the holon index and home page. Each holon page now carries more of its own detail rather than a placeholder summary.

9EPhR corrected to its authoritative list

The Nine Essential Physical Requirements framework was corrected to its authoritative nine-item list (Water, Food, Shelter, Energy, Transportation, Hygiene & Healthcare, Clothing, Security, Digital). Clothing and Hygiene & Healthcare were then swapped so the rows trace a rough historical progression, and a “Learn more — the eras of human need” disclosure was added, distinguishing the Homo erectus base from pre-industrial additions and modern guarantees, and requirement from standard.

Complete Chronology opens

A new concept publication opened in Publications: Complete Chronology. Most knowledge systems keep what was thought and lose when — and what it followed from. Complete Chronology keeps one timeline across every chat, note, and thought as the primary structure, with pages as views into it and semantic zoom on top. A working intention, honestly partial.
