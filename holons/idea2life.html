01 — Philosophy

Bring Ideas to Life

idea2.life is EvoBioSys's venture-studio holon — the public vessel and business layer where a founder's vision becomes a living operational system. Its canonical self-definition: a recursive, human-guided, AI-augmented creative architecture that seamlessly outfolds visionary signal into living operational systems, catalyzing regenerative ventures. The engine that does this work — the Wondermachine — lives inside idea2.life as a sub-holon: idea2.life is the public vessel and the business; the Wondermachine is the machine inside it.

An idea, in this frame, is not a lottery ticket — it is a seed. It carries its own pattern, wants soil, roots, seasons, and it grows best in the hands of the person it is most aligned with. idea2.life exists to meet a founder's idea at that seed stage and walk it, stage by stage, toward a living venture.

A Fractal Co-Founder

idea2.life partners with a founder to develop their good-enough implementation to about 80% production-ready — sovereign, self-hostable infrastructure, shared craft, honest feedback — then hands full stewardship back to them. No capture, no lock-in. Technology serves the venture and its founder, not the other way around.

The project carries a dedicated Tana workspace with its own supertag (i2L idea2Life), a published OnePager, and a growing body of documentation under the idea2Life unboxing supertag — a collection of discovery moments in the craft of building ventures from ideas.

02 — Service Tiers

The Service Cube

Every stage of the idea-to-life pipeline is offered across three service tiers. Each tier handles a distinct kind of founder need; together they form the cube that funds the venture engine without making it dependent on any single revenue line.

Tier 01
DIY

Open Source

Free, self-serve blueprints and templates — the top of the funnel, not a direct revenue line. A replicability play: a founder who wants to build alongside idea2.life without a contract can.

  • Open-source project templates and runbooks
  • Default build stack: lovable + supabase + github/codeberg + netlify, with base44 as the cheap first tier
  • No gatekeeping — anyone can start here
Tier 02
Platform

Self-Serve, AI-Managed

The idea2Life Platform offer — value-based pricing on the premium, AI-managed layer of the pipeline.

  • AI-augmented guidance through each pipeline stage
  • Value-based pricing (confirmed model, 2026-07-02)
  • Open-source plus premium — the DIY tier stays free underneath it
Tier 03
Partner

Concierge, High-Touch Human

Fractional-expert playbooks and hands-on collaboration, priced as a percentage or success-share rather than a flat fee. A personal Concierge tier — "one removed" — is available at a discount from the standard company terms.

  • Fractional-expert playbooks, company IP, percentage/success-share
  • Personal Concierge tier, discounted vs. company terms
  • Incubator success-sharing once a spawned venture graduates
Idea-to-Life Pipeline
03 — Pipeline

The Wondermachine Pipeline

A founder's vision moves through the Wondermachine pipeline in stages — each one a gate, not a formality. Evidence, not narrative, is what moves an idea forward.

01

Intake

The vision arrives. A founder profile is confirmed, and the idea is met by the engine and its human stewards.

02

Articulate

Core Signal becomes a one-pager or landing page. Fed by the VisionCasting funnel — idea2Spiral's threshold session, run with Manuel Spornberger.

03

Prototype

A vibe-coded MVP takes shape, built on the default stack: lovable, supabase, and github/codeberg, with base44 or Cursor for the cheap first tier.

04

Validate

Demand gets tested before more gets built — the same gate discipline the engine asks of every venture, applied honestly, not skipped.

05

Spread

The venture publishes and finds its first users through the Publication Pipeline. See what's already alive at idea2.site.

Two further stages — Teaming Up (co-creator matching) and Incorporate (legal scaffolding and funding decision) — complete a founder's path to a fully spun-up venture. Both show as "notify me" on the live funnel today — not yet built, named honestly rather than overclaimed.

04 — Connections

People & Holons

idea2.life does not exist in isolation. Ventures spawned through the Wondermachine pipeline graduate into their own holons once they clear the Incorporate gate. Manuel Spornberger is idea2.life's referral partner through idea2Spiral (i2S) — his "Engine Room" methodology feeds the VisionCasting funnel into idea2.life's Articulate stage, on a contract-bound 10% referral.

The holon connects to the EvoBioSys Publication Pipeline for spreading finished ventures, and to kidur for the open-source tooling philosophy that underpins every build. Already-living ventures — Flowtunes, Purpose Pilot, Unlocking Housing, and others — are published at idea2.site.
